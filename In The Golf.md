# In The ~~Groove~~ Golf

## What?

A golf themed event, featuring a varied collection of courses with 18 charts each, a front/back 9 course (via course mode in game), and a full 18 chart course. This means we have competitive leaderboards

1. For the pack overall (sum of best individual charts)  
2. For each chart in the pack  
3. For the front 9 course  
4. For the back 9 course  
5. For the full 18 course

## Themes

Each course gets it’s own theme of sorts, which the curator for that course will work with us (event insiders) to define with a couple constraints:

1. Courses need to be relatively constrained in difficulty block range so that the in game courses for front/back 9 and full 18 actually are fun.  
2. They must have some general cohesive theme so we can solicit charts for it. This could be anything, low tech, high tech, stamina, stamtech, xo focused, burfus, jurfus, lowers, uppers, mods, gimmicks, tournament coded, indie rock, foreign artists, etc. etc.  
3. Charts must be **unreleased only.** We have no shortage of charter volunteers (\~35).  
4. Content must be fun on not CMOD. The event **will not allow CMOD for scoring.**

## Curators

We will tap some initial volunteers for curation.

The curators get to solicit the content they want to curate and the event insiders will work with them to provide support and organization in the form of project management and tooling built on Arrow Cloud. 

## Charters

Charters will be solicited for charts, and given feedback from curators on their submissions, but they will not be exposed to full insider information regarding curation status, other submissions, etc. This way charters get to have fun with the event too. They can keep their submissions private if they want or share with other charters, that’s fine. 

## Scoring

Golf scoring \- get your score as close to zero as possible. An example could be that you are given one “stroke” per total second of your combined absolute offsets. Said another way, if your mean absolute error on a chart is 5.00ms, and the chart has 1000 steps, your score is 5 (5,000ms \-\> 5s). 

This likely needs some thinking yet for weighting (maybe all hits within 5ms of 0 count as 0, for example), and misses need to be balanced appropriately.

Par can be applied to both songs, and the courses overall, the par numbers would be our guess at an “average” score, then people can get birdies, eagles, bogies, etc. based on their performance.

## Stamina/StamTech Curation (name tbd)

Discord channel ID: 1500503650714521670

**Stamina (Vanilla):**
- Difficulty 11-13; bpm range 120-165; length: 2:30-7:00 (soft cutoffs, please message if outside this range). Be conscious of the difficulty of the chart; for instance, a 7 minute long dense song at 120 bpm is not particularly fun for a lot of people. A 2:45 125 bpm broken stream 11 is pretty fun for most people. Think Crab Rave 12 > Colors of Love 12.
- High priority of variety music (bangers only style)
- 32 measures minimum total stream in chart.
- No tech allowed.

**Stamtech:**
- Difficulty 11-13; bpm range 120-165; length 2:30-7:00.
- 32 measures of stream recommended in chart. Does not have to be unbroken to count.
- Tech is allowed in streams and in breaks. Song does not have to be a singular bpm.
- Allowed tech: FS, BR, XO-, SJ, SS-, BU, JA, KS-, RS (roll switch or roll stream), MA, DS, HT
- Please keep chained tech to a minimum. The idea is to keep a healthy lifebar and have fun. Four 16th note brackets are fine, 2+ measures of bracket stream is not fine.

Cool motifs are extremely important in these charts because stamina can be boring! Bring a theme to the song, whether a unique pattern or chart design setup. The charts should not have “a part” (choke points) or sections that can easily fail someone that would normally be able to pass that block difficulty. Charts may start at beat 0, however, please keep in mind that will negate potential break time, and also hot starts are very hard on timing and not always welcome.
